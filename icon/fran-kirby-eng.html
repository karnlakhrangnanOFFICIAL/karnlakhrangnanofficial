Career & Highlights

Francesca Kirby joined from Reading in 2015 and established herself as the creative heartbeat of Emma Hayes' Chelsea attack. Renowned for her exceptional dribbling, vision, and clinical finishing.

She won both the PFA Players' Player of the Year and FWA Footballer of the Year awards twice, helping Chelsea secure 6 WSL titles, 5 FA Cups, and cementing her legacy as the club’s all-time leading goalscorer with 115 goals in 208 appearances.
