Career & Highlights

Ji So-yun (지소연) signed from INAC Kobe Leonessa in 2014 and made history as the first non-British player to win the PFA Women's Players' Player of the Year award (2015).

She scored the winning goal in the 2015 Women's FA Cup Final to secure the club's first major trophy and is widely regarded as one of the most technically gifted midfielders in league history.
