Career & Highlights

Magdalena Lilly Eriksson is a left-footed centre-back who took over the captain's armband from Karen Carney in 2019. Renowned for her leadership, tactical awareness, and progressive passing from the back.

She guided the team to multiple domestic doubles and trebles, led Chelsea to the 2021 UEFA Women's Champions League Final, and cemented her status as one of the finest defenders and captains in club history before joining Bayern Munich in 2023.
